# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AIP a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and respect  
- Focusing on what is best for the scientific and engineering community  
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback  
- Respecting ethical boundaries of the project (no pressure to weaken medical disclaimers)  

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or discriminatory comments  
- Publishing others’ private information  
- Bad-faith scientific sabotage or fabricated results in contributions  
- Pushing clinical/diagnostic features despite documented non-goals  

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards.  
Report concerns via the security/conduct contact listed in project governance (to be designated publicly at first open release).
